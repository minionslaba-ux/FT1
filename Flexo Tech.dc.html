01 — About

A regional hub at the crossroads of Europe and Asia.

We import, store and resell chemical raw materials for industrial consumers across the CIS, Eastern Europe and emerging markets in the Middle East and Asia.

Georgia's free trade agreements with the EU and China let us operate as an efficient distribution point, with product held in licensed third-party chemical warehouses in Georgia and partner markets.
